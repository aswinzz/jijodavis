# Updating your website

Everything on jijodavis.com can be changed from your phone. You don't need a computer or any technical knowledge.

## Signing in (first time only)

1. Open **jijodavis.com/admin** in Safari or Chrome.
2. Tap **Sign In with GitHub** and sign in with your GitHub account.
3. Tip: tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. The editor then opens like an app.

You stay signed in afterwards.

## Adding client transformations (one or many)

1. Tap **Photos** → **Client transformations**.
2. In the **Photos** box, tap **Browse**, then **Upload**, and select as many photos from your camera roll as you like.
3. Tap **Save** (top right).

The website updates about a minute later, and the new photos appear at the top of the gallery.

Photos are shrunk automatically, so upload them straight from your camera roll. Before-and-after photos work best already joined side by side (most collage apps do this).

## Changing the order or removing a photo

The website shows the **last** photo in the list **first**, which is why new uploads appear at the top.

- **Show a photo earlier on the website:** move it further **down** the list, with **Move Down** or the **⋯** menu → Move to Bottom.
- **Remove a photo:** tap the **✕** next to it, then **Save**.

The first 12 photos show on the website straight away. The rest appear when visitors tap "Show more".

## Adding a caption to a photo

Captions are optional. Under **Captions**, tap **Add caption**, pick the photo, type the caption (e.g. *12 weeks, 9 kg down*) and tap **Save**.

## Adding a certificate

**Photos** → **Certificates** → **Add certificate**. Upload the photo, fill in the title, who issued it and the year, then **Save**.

## Changing text

**Page text** has a page for each part of the website: Top of page, About Jijo, Coaching services, How it works, Questions (FAQ) and Contact form. Change anything, then tap **Save**.

- **Bright words in headings:** put them between stars. `Real clients. *Real results.*` shows "Real results." in bright white.
- **Lists** (services, FAQ questions, steps): use **Add** for a new one and **⋯** to move or remove one.

## Contact details

**Settings** → **Contact details, Google & menu** covers:
- your WhatsApp number (digits only, with the country code, e.g. 971501234567)
- your phone, email and Instagram
- the areas you train in
- the text Google shows for your website
- which sections appear on the page, and in what order

## Something looks wrong?

- **Changes not showing?** Wait 2 minutes, then pull down to refresh the page.
- **Made a mistake?** Change it back and tap **Save** again. Every earlier version is kept, so nothing is ever lost.
- **Stuck?** Message Aswin.
